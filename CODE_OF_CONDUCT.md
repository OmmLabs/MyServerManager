# Code of conduct

Keep project discussions welcoming and focused on the work. Treat participants respectfully, explain disagreements with concrete examples and respect other people's time and privacy.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and posting another person's private details are unacceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may remove harmful content or limit participation based on the behavior and its impact.

Report conduct concerns privately to a listed maintainer through the contact supplied with the release. Include the relevant link or context and let the maintainer know how to reach you. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and avoid conflicts of interest.
